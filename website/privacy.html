Privacy Policy for Course Finder

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Course Finder helps users discover Art of Living programs across India.

Information We Collect

Course Finder does not require users to create an account.

The app may access device location only if you grant permission, to show nearby programs.

How We Use Information

Location is used only to show nearby Art of Living courses and improve search results. Your precise location is not stored on our servers.

Course Information

Course information displayed in the app is obtained from publicly available Art of Living program listings and may change over time.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play Services, Google Chrome Trusted Web Activity, Cloudflare Pages, Google Analytics and OpenStreetMap map tiles.

Data Sharing

Course Finder does not sell or share your personal information.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to keep the application secure. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Course Finder is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
